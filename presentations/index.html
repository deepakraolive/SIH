THE PROBLEM · SIH 26091 Grassroots Entrepreneurship Reality
GramVest | Smart India Hackathon 2026

Problem Statement

The government provides 90% concessional credit with only 10% beneficiary margin. Yet, thousands of rural micro-enterprises stagnate or default within Year 1 due to two fatal blind spots: zero localized market intelligence and intimidating financial illiteracy.

1
No Local Market Visibility
Urban-centric research tools completely ignore villages. Prospective entrepreneurs cannot estimate 5–10km catchment demand, seasonal price dips, or customer purchasing power.
2
Competition is Invisible
Beneficiaries select trades based on anecdotal hearsay. Opening the 5th kirana store or dairy stall in a single hamlet triggers instant market saturation and price wars.
3
Financing is Confusing
Calculating 10% margin equity, 90% debt eligibility, Micro Finance (≤ ₹1.4L @ 6.5%) vs Term Loans (≤ ₹50L @ 8%), moratorium math, and bank DPR preparation is intimidating.
4
High Failure, Zero Guidance
Formal business consultancies charge ₹15,000–₹50,000+ and don't operate in rural blocks. Without guidance, marginalized youth sink their life savings and default.
THE BOTTLENECK: Capital is available via SCAs and CAs, but institutional-grade feasibility intelligence has never reached the village doorstep.

AI
AI, GIS & Spatial Data
PostgreSQL + PostGIS pgvector / FAISS LangChain / RAG OpenStreetMap / Mandi API
